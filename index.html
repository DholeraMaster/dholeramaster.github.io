Dholera Land Glossary

The terms you'll hear when buying land in Dholera SIR, explained in plain English. Written by DholeraMaster, an independent Dholera land research and advisory practice.

7/12 extract (Satbara Utara)

Gujarat's basic land record for a survey number. It shows who owns the land, its area, how it is classified and any rights or loans recorded against it. The seller's name on the 7/12 should match the person selling to you.

See the full checklist before buying land in Dholera.

8-A extract

The holding (khata) record that lists every survey number held by one account holder. Reading it alongside the 7/12 helps confirm that the seller actually holds the parcel being offered.

NA conversion (non-agricultural)

Official permission to use agricultural land for non-agricultural purposes such as housing or industry. "NA in process" is not the same as NA granted, so ask for the order itself.

TP scheme (Town Planning scheme)

A plan that pools land in an area, lays out roads and public amenities, and returns a reshaped plot to each owner. Dholera SIR is divided into TP schemes, and the scheme and zone of a plot largely decide what can be built on it and what it is worth.

View all 16 Dholera TP scheme maps.

Original plot (OP) and final plot (FP)

The original plot is the land before the TP scheme. The final plot is what the owner receives afterwards, usually smaller, because part of the land goes to roads and amenities, but with planned access. Always ask which final plot number a parcel maps to.

Dholera SIR (Special Investment Region)

The planned region notified by the Gujarat government for Dholera's industrial and urban development. Land inside the SIR boundary falls under its TP schemes; land outside it does not, even if it is marketed as "Dholera."

Compare land inside vs outside Dholera SIR.

DSIRDA

The Dholera Special Investment Region Development Authority, the body that plans and regulates development within Dholera SIR, including its TP schemes.

DICDL

Dholera Industrial City Development Limited, the company set up to build Dholera's core infrastructure such as roads, water, drainage and power.

Read the Dholera infrastructure status for 2026.

Activation Area

The first part of Dholera SIR to be developed, where trunk infrastructure and the first industrial allotments are concentrated. It is the most expensive land in the region.

See the Dholera land price trend from 2016 to 2026.

CRZ (Coastal Regulation Zone)

Areas near the coast and tidal creeks where national rules restrict construction. Parts of the Dholera region are close to the Gulf of Khambhat, so check whether a plot is affected before buying.

Learn more about CRZ in Dholera SIR.

Jantri rate

The Gujarat government's reference rate for land, used to calculate stamp duty and registration charges. Market prices can sit well above or below it.

AnyRoR

Gujarat's free online portal for viewing land records, including the 7/12 and 8-A. Any buyer can check a survey number there before paying.

Open AnyRoR, or get an independent legal title check.

Power of Attorney (PoA)

A registered document that lets someone act for you, often used by NRIs to complete a purchase without travelling. Give it only to someone you trust, and limit it to the specific transaction.

See the NRI guide to buying land in Dholera.

Bigha and square yard

Dholera plots are usually priced per square yard, while farmland is often described in bigha, a unit whose size varies by area. Convert carefully before comparing prices.
